# Extrabits Junior – Basic Website Information

## About Extrabits Junior

**Extrabits Junior** is an IT coaching and computer training institute located in Surat, Gujarat, specially designed for students from **Class 8th to 12th** who want to build their future in computers and technology.

We focus on helping school students learn computer basics, coding fundamentals, and practical IT skills in an easy and student-friendly way. Our courses are designed to make learning simple, interactive, and career-oriented.

From basic computer knowledge to programming and web development foundations, Extrabits Junior helps students gain confidence in the digital world from an early age.

For advanced professional IT courses, students can continue learning through our main academy website:

🌐 [Extrabits Academy](https://www.extrabitsacademy.com/)

---

# Our Mission

Our mission is to empower school students with modern computer and IT skills that help them grow academically and prepare for future careers in technology.

We aim to create a practical and engaging learning environment where students can explore coding, creativity, and digital skills with confidence.
